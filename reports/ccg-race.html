How these projections work

We use Tuesday's projected scores before kickoff, then update with live points and estimated remaining scores (with their ± uncertainty). We simulate many possible finishes, ranking teams by record, points for, then points against. Team scores are modeled independently, even when their NFL players share a game. All results use NCAA 180 standard scoring. If teams remain exactly tied after points against, a real coin toss decides the division. Unresolved coin-toss probability is divided equally among tied teams; no winner is assumed.
