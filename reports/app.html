Notifications

Get a notification when your teams' games are final. No account, no login. Tester phone

First: add this app to your Home Screen

On iPhone, notifications only work once the app is on your Home Screen.

  1. In Safari, tap the Share button.
  2. Choose Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Open NCAA 180 from your Home Screen and come back to this page.
