How Legend Score works

Points for what a manager's teams achieved, added up over every season.

  • Regular-season win (tie = ½)1
  • Unbeaten regular season (11-0)+5
  • Division title (CCG appearance)3
  • Conference title (CCG win)+5
  • Bowl win (incl. 2020 third-place game)2
  • NIT champion / runner-up4 / 2
  • Playoff appearance5
  • Reached Sweet 16+3
  • Reached Elite 8+4
  • Reached Final Four+6
  • Reached title game+8
  • National title+25

Playoff steps add up: a national champion earns 5 + 3 + 4 + 6 + 8 + 25 = 51 from the playoffs. 2019 started at the Sweet 16, so every 2019 playoff team gets appearance + Sweet 16. 2020's group stage counts as the Sweet 16 step, earned as 3 × the share of group rivals outscored; group winners then reached the Elite 8.

Other columns

All-play %: share of every other team in NCAA 180 outscored each week. Lineup Skill: how close each lineup came to the best possible, from 0 (worst lineup you could have started) to 1 (best possible), pooled over every game; the worst lineup only counts players who actually scored. Left on bench / g: best possible lineup minus points started, per game. Beat proj / g (2026 on): points started minus what the lineup Sleeper's kickoff projections said to start would have scored.

Managers are tracked by Sleeper account, shown under their latest username. Regular-season games count for whoever owned the team that week; postseason results go to the owner at the end of that regular season. Before 2026, each season is credited to the owner at season's end. Orphaned (caretaker or vacant) teams are excluded. 2019–2025 points use one standard scoring system; results stand as played.
