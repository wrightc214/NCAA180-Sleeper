How it works

8Computer bots

The poll

  • Every bot ranks every team in all leagues, using regular-season weeks 1–11.
  • One scale. Each bot's rating is turned into standard deviations from the average team (z-score, capped at ±3).
  • Best and worst dropped. For each team, its single best and single worst bot are thrown out (BCS style); the rest are averaged. That average is the Score: 0 = average team, +1 = one standard deviation better.
  • Top 25 = the 25 highest Scores. Ties: Score, then average bot rank, then Scoreboard.
  • First-place votes = how many bots rank the team #1.
  • Others receiving votes = outside the top 25 but in at least one counted bot's top 25.
  • Missing data is bridged, not left blank. A bot missing a week's input uses a straight line between the nearest weeks with data, or the nearest week's value when there is only one side; those ballots are marked * on the poll page. A bot with no data at all for a season sits out (shown –) and the other bots carry the poll.
  • Final poll (after the postseason): postseason games count as extra games for Scoreboard, Bracketologist, Monday Morning QB; Vegas, Bandwagon, Headliner, Hammer, Bagman, Professor stay at the end of the regular season.
  • Postseason bumps added to Score per win: playoff rounds +0.15/+0.25/+0.45/+0.85/+1.65, conference title game +0.15, bowl +0.15, NIT +0.05 (× share of rivals outscored).
  • Final-four backstop: a semifinal or final winner always ranks directly above the team it beat.

The bots

ScoreboardSCB

“I only care about the standings.”

Measures: Exactly the playoff rank: wins, then points for

Inputs: Wins (ties = half), then official total points as the tiebreaker - the league's real playoff rank. Regular season only.

VegasVEG

“Strip out the schedule luck and tell me who is actually good.”

Measures: Expected wins vs. every other team, every week

Inputs: Each week, the share of all 180 teams you outscored. Summed over the season; points for breaks ties.

BandwagonBAND

“Whoever is hot right now.”

Measures: All-play over the last 3 weeks, weighted 3:2:1

Inputs: All-play share over the last 3 weeks, weighted 3:2:1 (most recent heaviest).

BracketologistBRKT

“Who you beat matters as much as how many.”

Measures: Win % plus opponents' all-play strength

Inputs: 60% win % + 40% average all-play strength of the opponents you played. Weeks 1-11 only.

HeadlinerHEAD

“Star power: the most points this roster could have put up.”

Measures: Best possible lineup per game

Inputs: Average points per week of your best possible lineup from your own roster.

HammerHAM

“Close wins don't impress me.”

Measures: How hard you win (and lose), ranked against that week's games

Inputs: Each game: a win scores where its margin ranks among that week's winning margins (biggest blowout +1, median win +0.5, a squeaker about 0); a loss scores minus the same for that week's losing margins; a tie 0. Season value = the average. Regular season only.

BagmanBAG

“Follows the money: what the market says your starters are worth.”

Measures: Redraft market value of the best starting lineup (FantasyCalc)

Inputs: FantasyCalc redraft value of your best legal starting lineup, from the latest value snapshot. Before 2026 (no market history): Sleeper's projected starter points for the next week.
