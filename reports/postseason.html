How the projection works

Every remaining regular-season game (weeks 9–11) and every conference championship game goes to the team with the higher season scoring average. Then the real postseason rules run on those results: conference champions get automatic bids (AQ), at-large spots go by record and the tiebreakers, and bowl teams are ordered by poll rank adjusted for wins (25 poll spots per win). Projected seeds use the latest poll, adjusted for projected wins and losses; the real seeds come from the bots' seeding ballot after championship week. Marquee Bowls give each league a seat (the better of its two division leaders still available), then fill by bowl order. Records shown are projected final records.
