How trade grades work

Grades use FantasyCalc values available at trade time — not a verdict. Rebuild includes rookie picks and can discount value spread across assets. For smaller trades, displayed grade ranges narrow based on combined raw value relative to the snapshot leader for that grade. Point totals don’t change. Contender uses raw rest-of-season values and values traded picks at zero. Unlisted kickers and D/ST use a 4.12 proxy in both grades. Surplus pick comparisons use the upcoming draft at trade time. Blockbuster marks a large raw dynasty deal with a high-value player, both measured against the snapshot’s top player. It does not indicate a winner. Team Fit compares lineup gains and future value versus holding for each team. It is the primary grade. The two market grades use quieter colors based on each team’s win-now weight, without changing their letters or points. A hyphen means evidence is missing or an after-trade snapshot is still pending. Projected wins and before/after odds are unavailable.
